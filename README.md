# 29sqn-leave-app

NZCF Unit Leave reporting app



A rather over-engineered way to replace a Google Form, but more fun.



Concept:



A person files a leave form.



This triggers notification emails to people on an approved list.



Anyone with "adjutant" powers can log into the app and see 

upcoming leave, past leave, and leave taken by people in total

in a more understandable way than just having a list.



Project was started for the author to freshen old and pick up

new skills.  As-is-where-is, but in use at author's own unit.
